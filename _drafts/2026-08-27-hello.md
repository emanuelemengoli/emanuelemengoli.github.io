I am starting this space to keep informal notes alongside my research on
stochastic modelling of wireless networks. The plan is to write short pieces
on things that are useful to me and might be useful to others:

- **Derivations and intuition** — point-process identities, coverage and
  interference computations, and the small lemmas that never make it into a
  paper but take an afternoon to re-derive.
- **Simulation notes** — how I set up experiments for non-terrestrial
  networks, what breaks, and reproducible snippets.
- **Paper walkthroughs** — summaries of work I am reading, with the parts I
  found hard spelled out.

Posts will be irregular and deliberately rough. If something here is wrong or
unclear, corrections are welcome.
